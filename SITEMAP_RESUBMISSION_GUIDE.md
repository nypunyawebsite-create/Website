## 📝 How to Resubmit (Quick Steps)

### Method 1: Resubmit Existing Sitemap
1. Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Click **Sitemaps** (left sidebar)
3. Find `sitemap.xml` in the list
4. Click **Resubmit** button (if available)
5. Or click **Test** then **Submit**

### Method 2: Remove and Re-add
1. Go to **Sitemaps** section
2. Find `sitemap.xml`
3. Click **Remove** (or delete icon)
4. Enter `sitemap.xml` again
5. Click **Submit**

### Method 3: Use URL Inspection (For Specific Pages)
1. Use **URL Inspection** tool
2. Enter each service page URL
3. Click **Request Indexing**
4. Do this for top 10-15 pages
